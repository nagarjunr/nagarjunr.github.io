You can lead without managing people.

By Nagarjun Rajendran / 15 January 2026

Nobody told me that my biggest leadership moment would be saying "no" in a meeting where I had zero authority.

Senior technical roles get misunderstood often. People assume leadership means direct reports and performance reviews. It does not always work that way.

In my role, leadership looks different:

  • Owning architecture decisions when no one else will.
  • Saying no to designs that will not scale, even when they are the easier choice.
  • Helping teams think through tradeoffs instead of handing them solutions.
  • Being the escalation point when problems cross boundaries and no one knows who should fix them.

No direct reports. Still plenty of responsibility.

This kind of leadership is quiet. You are not in the spotlight. But over time, it shapes how systems are built and how teams work together. Especially in complex environments where decisions ripple across multiple projects.

If you are an individual contributor in a technical role, how do you think about leadership? What does it look like for you?

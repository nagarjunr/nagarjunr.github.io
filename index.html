My core is Technology Solutions Architecture in IoT, Cloud and Applied AI. My wider interests are practical GenAI and technical leadership. I prefer clear explanations, visible tradeoffs, and ideas tested against real constraints.

ClarityPracticalityContinuous learning

I work with customers and teams to understand technical choices and build a path forward. I work at Robert Bosch LLC in Illinois as a Cloud and Technology Solutions Manager, Expert. My focus spans IoT and cloud architecture, GenAI use cases, customer education, technology consultation, and proofs of concept.

I care about the questions behind a solution: What problem matters? What will happen when something fails? Can the people who own it understand how it works? I see technical leadership as creating clarity, supporting experts, and helping others make sound decisions. I also enjoy sharing practical learning and offering peer guidance based on my own technology journey.

Two habits shape how I work. I listen closely, which helps me uncover patterns and connect the dots in complex situations. I plan carefully and in detail, which brings meaningful insights to the surface and supports data-driven decisions that make a real impact.
